# Webcam eye tracking evidence

Visual orientation describes where the eyes and head are directed. Covert visual attention can shift without eye movement. Engagement concerns participation in the task. Workload concerns cognitive demand and effort. Fatigue concerns reduced capacity or alertness over time. Psychological stress concerns appraisal and response to demands. Arousal is activation that can accompany positive or negative experiences. A person can be engaged while looking away and can face the screen while mind wandering.

| Evidence | Design and labels | Findings and transfer to this game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Laut et al., *Scientific Reports* 16 (2026). [PubMed and figures](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42323438/), [article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13283211/) | VR goalkeeper and simulated job interview. Calibrated VR gaze and pupil diameter versus uncalibrated interview pupil recordings from a 25 Hz Pupil Labs device. Interview labels drew on self-report, interview content and cortisol. Five-second windows, random forest on engineered pupil/fixation features and CNN/LSTM/ConvLSTM on pupil or gaze-derived time series. Nested leave-one-subject-out validation. | Macro F1 dropped materially in the interview dataset. Hardware, signals and tasks differed, so this comparison cannot isolate a single cause or demonstrate ordinary webcam validity. The numerical headline in the indexed abstract is rendered as an embedded formula. The methods and directional result were recoverable through PubMed and indexed full text after the publisher redirected to authentication. |
| B. Gorin et al., *Sensors* 24(6), 1759 (2024). [Full text](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/24/6/1759) | Review of gamified and simulated sensorimotor tasks, including driving, games, and rehabilitation. Studies used pupil/gaze/fixation/saccade measures alongside difficulty manipulations, performance and self-report such as NASA-TLX. No common train/test split or classifier. | Pupil changes often tracked effort and arousal, but associations varied with task and could be nonlinear. The review gives no pooled classifier accuracy. Retain eye features as observations and avoid mapping them directly to workload or stress. |
| C. Hutt et al., *Behavior Research Methods* 56, 1–17 (2024). [Full-text PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/s13428-022-02040-x.pdf) | Two remote reading studies with calibrated WebGazer (105 students and 173 online recruits). Thought probes labeled task-unrelated thought (TUT), and response correctness labeled comprehension. Dispersion/gaze counts and task-specific areas of interest fed supervised classifiers, compared with chance and interaction baselines. The authors also evaluated cross-study transfer. | Best combined TUT model (XGBoost) had κ=.15 and TUT F1=.25. For correctness, the best task-local XGBoost model had κ=.57 and error-class F1=.60. Drift and stronger results from task-specific features limit transfer. The app retains coarse relative direction and dispersion without inferring what a player is thinking. |
| D. *Monitoring Learner Attention in Online Education Using Webcam-based Eye Tracking*, ICETT 2026. [Publisher abstract](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11666300) | Browser MediaPipe plus mouse activity and emotion recognition in a weighted fusion dashboard. Single participant, about 23 minutes. Full methods and independent labels could not be verified from the accessible abstract. | Reported 30 FPS, mean score 72.3 and three fatigue events. These describe operation and outputs. Use local processing and measure responsiveness separately from construct validity. |
| E. *Pupil Response in Visual Tracking Tasks*, *Sensors* 24(8), 2545 (2024). [Full-text PDF](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/24/8/2545/pdf?version=1713254897), [indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38676162/) | Fifteen adults repeated arrow-tracking at five complexity levels, using a 60 Hz Tobii Pro Nano, standardized lighting and stabilized head position. Baseline-corrected pupil size, recall accuracy and NASA-TLX. Repeated-measures ANOVA and mixed effects. | Repetition reduced pupil size with diminishing effect at higher load. The main load effect approached significance, gaze region had no significant effect, and the workload association was inverted-U. The lab used dedicated hardware and controlled conditions. The game reports no webcam pupillometry. |
| Krafka et al., CVPR 2016. [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/html/Krafka_Eye_Tracking_for_CVPR_2016_paper.html) | GazeCapture, 1,450+ people and roughly 2.5M mobile images with displayed gaze targets. iTracker CNN uses eyes, face and a face-location grid. | Calibration reduced reported error from 1.71 to 1.34 cm on phones and 2.53 to 2.12 cm on tablets. The original app ran at 10–15 FPS on then-current mobile devices. Device geometry and a learned model matter for screen coordinates, so a center calibration alone provides orientation only. |
| Barrett et al., *Psychological Science in the Public Interest* (2019). [Review](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/1529100619832930) | Review of production and perception of facial movements across people and contexts. No single classifier performance metric. | Facial movements are context-dependent. The legacy weighted score is explicitly experimental. Blendshapes remain available as descriptive inputs. |

## Technology choice

| Approach | Accuracy and calibration | Runtime, integration, and maintenance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MediaPipe Tasks Face Landmarker and iris geometry | Reuses tracked mesh/iris points. Center reference aligns relative geometry to the player. Screen coordinates need a separate per-display mapping and validation. | Existing Python and OpenCV path ran on arm64 with a CPU delegate. Tasks has Python and web APIs. Apache-2.0 framework. Legacy Face Mesh/Iris documentation is useful for geometry, while Tasks provides the existing blendshape and transform outputs. [Tasks API](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker/python), [Iris](https://research.google/blog/mediapipe-iris-real-time-iris-tracking-depth-estimation/), [repository](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe) |
| OpenCV | Capture, rendering and linear algebra. A standalone traditional pupil/eye detector would need separate validation across light and reflections. | Existing native Python dependency on arm64, with CPU operations for this project. OpenCV.js is a separate browser runtime with browser camera APIs. [License and repository](https://github.com/opencv/opencv) |
| WebGazer.js | Browser regression fitted with user interactions or calibration targets. Supported by reading studies with task-specific limitations. | Browser-native, no Python integration in the same process. Runtime depends on model, webcam and browser. Official maintenance ended and the project lists GPLv3 with a conditional LGPL licensing option. [Project](https://github.com/brownhci/WebGazer) |
| iTracker/GazeCapture | Learned screen prediction studied on mobile geometry. Needs laptop validation and likely recalibration. | Adds a second model and preprocessing. Porting original research code or exporting it for browser or acceleration requires separate engineering, benchmarking, and license review for code, data and weights. [Project](https://gazecapture.csail.mit.edu/) |

For this application, reuse Face Landmarker and publish relative orientation, observed time, blinks and geometry. Use center orientation for optional gaze-driven game interactions, with validity gating. Saccade classification needs faster, validated sampling. Stress inference needs independently labeled sessions and evaluation on held-out sessions and participants. The source includes a separate historical stress heuristic and personal-model path for existing gameplay clients.
